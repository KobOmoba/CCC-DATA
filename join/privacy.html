Privacy notice

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

What this page collects

Opening this page collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no cookies, no tracking, no advertising. Our hosting provider may keep short technical logs (such as IP address and time of visit) to keep the site secure.

Member sign-up ("Join us"): if you choose to register, the details you type (name, phone, date of birth, optional email, address, next of kin and photo) go only to this parish's admin, with your consent, to create your membership record. They are never shown on this page. The parish admin may reject or delete a request; you may ask the parish to correct or erase your data at any time.

What this page shows

Only the sign-up form. No member's personal data is ever shown here. Member records are kept privately by each parish, with the member's consent, in a separate secured system.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to be informed, to access, correct or erase your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). If you are a member, ask your parish about the data it holds on you.

Data controller

This parish of the Celestial Church of Christ. For privacy questions, contact your parish admin.
