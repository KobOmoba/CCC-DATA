Privacy notice

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

What this website collects

Nothing about you. This website has no sign-in, no forms, no cookies, no tracking and no advertising. Our hosting provider may keep short technical logs (such as IP address and time of visit) to keep the site secure.

What this website shows

Only public church information: parish names, cities, countries, map links and service times, plus events and sermons the church has chosen to publish. No member's personal data is ever shown here. Member records are kept privately by each parish, with the member's consent, in a separate secured system.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to be informed, to access, correct or erase your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). If you are a member, ask your parish about the data it holds on you.

Data controller

Celestial Church of Christ World-wide. For privacy questions, use the contact page.
