About & beliefs

The Celestial Church of Christ (CCC) is a Christian church founded on 29 September 1947 in Porto-Novo, in present-day Benin Republic, by the Reverend Samuel Bilewu Joseph Oshoffa. From West Africa it has grown into a worldwide church with parishes across Africa, Europe, the Americas and Asia.

What we believe

One God

We worship one God: Father, Son and Holy Spirit, and we believe in Jesus Christ as Lord and Saviour.

The Holy Bible

The Bible is the Word of God and the guide for our faith and daily life.

Prayer and healing

We believe in the power of prayer, in spiritual healing and in the work of the Holy Spirit among believers.

Holiness

Members worship in white garments and barefoot, as a sign of purity and reverence in God's presence.

Our parishes

Each parish is led by its shepherd and serves its local community with weekly services, prayer meetings, choir, Sunday School and outreach.
